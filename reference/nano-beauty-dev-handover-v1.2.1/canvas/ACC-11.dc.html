Deposits

Treatments over $150 need a $50 deposit when you book. It comes off your bill on the day.

Changes and cancellations

Change or cancel free up to 48 hours before. Later than that, your deposit becomes clinic credit.

Missed visits

If you miss a visit without telling us, the deposit is kept.
