How booking works

We book through Fresha, the clinic’s booking system. Here’s what happens.

1. Pick your treatment hereBrowse, compare prices and read what to expect.
2. Choose a time in FreshaIt opens inside this app. Pay any deposit there.
3. See it in VisitsOnce Fresha shares it, your booking shows here with reminders and aftercare.
