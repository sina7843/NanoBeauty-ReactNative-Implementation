# BookingSummary

The review step before confirming: what, who, when, where, what's due now and later, and the policy (BOOK 06, PAY 05).

**Props:** `rows` (`{label, value}`), `total`, `dueNow`, `dueLater`, `policy`.

The amount due now is the largest figure on the screen, set in `amount`. Taxes, discounts and package or credit applied appear as their own lines when they exist (PAY 08). The deposit model and policy text are clinic-owned; placeholders say so. Sits on `surface`, never on a photo.
